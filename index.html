About Me

Partha Pratim Ray is an Indian academician, researcher, scientist, and prolific author, currently serving as an Assistant Professor (Stage II, Academic Level 11) in the Department of Computer Applications at Sikkim University, a Central University in Gangtok, India. Originally from Cooch Behar, West Bengal, he brings more than 14 years of experience in higher education, scientific research, and interdisciplinary innovation. His research spans the Internet of Things (IoT), Edge Artificial Intelligence, Large Language Models, Pervasive Generative AI, and Pervasive Biomedical Informatics. His work emphasizes intelligent, trustworthy, and resource-efficient computing systems for real-world applications.

An internationally recognized researcher, he has authored 189 journal publications, 38 conference papers, 6 published book chapters, and 11 books, with an additional book chapter accepted for publication. His scholarly contributions have attracted more than 15,500 Google Scholar citations, with an h-index of 43 and an i10-index of 95. He has been featured among the World's Top 2% Scientists in the Stanford University/Elsevier researcher rankings during 2020–2025. His professional distinctions include Fellowships of the Institution of Engineers in Scotland (FIES), Thinking About Thinking, and the Institution of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineers (IETE), alongside Senior Memberships of the Indian National Academy of Engineering (INAE) and IEEE. He has received several academic honors, including the IEI Young Engineers Award, Emerald Literati Award, and Sikkim University Publication Awards. He also serves in editorial capacities for international scholarly journals.

Beyond his contributions to science and technology, Ray is actively engaged in academic authorship, knowledge dissemination, and the preservation of India's intellectual and cultural heritage. He is the founder of भारतीय ज्ञान मंचः (Indian Knowledge Forum) , an initiative dedicated to promoting Indian Knowledge Systems. His interdisciplinary activities also encompass indigenous knowledge documentation and the digital preservation of Lepcha oral traditions in the Eastern Himalayas. Through research, teaching, publishing, and societal engagement, he seeks to bridge technological innovation, responsible scholarship, and civilizational knowledge for the advancement of science and society.
